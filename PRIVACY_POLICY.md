# Privacy policy

Atelier Contacts stores contact cleanup state, undo records, preferences, and vault exports on the device. The application does not include network permissions, advertising identifiers, analytics, crash telemetry, or cloud synchronization. The optional support button opens the user-selected browser at `https://app.lava.top/pharosteam`; no contact data is sent with that request.

Users control Android provider and calendar permissions. Removing the application removes its private database and preferences; exports created through the system document picker remain under the user's control.
